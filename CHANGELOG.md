# Changelog

## 7.0.4+16

- New roll styles: pick a wheel or a slot reel, and its colours.
- Remove & spin: take the pick out and spin the rest.
- Saved lists have their own tab; save any list with the bookmark.
- Choices with a comma, like "Tacos, burritos", save and load in one piece.
- Deleting a choice no longer deletes a saved list.
- Back clears a selection instead of closing the app.
- A cleaner, lighter look throughout.
